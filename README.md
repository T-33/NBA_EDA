## Exploratory Data Analysis of NBA players statistics from 1976 to 2017.

### Explored themes:
* Major stats (points, rebounds, assissts, steals, blocks) distribution across positions.
* Player efficiency metrics distribution (FG%, TS%, rTS%)
* How 3-point metrics (3pa, 3p%, 3pr) changed over the years, and how they are increasing during '3 point revolution'
